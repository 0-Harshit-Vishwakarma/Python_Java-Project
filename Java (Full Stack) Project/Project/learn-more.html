What is a carbon footprint?

It is an estimate of greenhouse-gas emissions connected with activities such as travel, energy use, food and purchases.

🚗

Transport

Car kilometres and flights can add emissions quickly. Public transport, shared rides and fewer unnecessary trips can help.

⚡

Home Energy

Electricity, gas and LPG use all contribute. Efficient appliances, less waste and renewable electricity can lower the impact.

🥗

Food & Diet

Different foods have different emission factors. Reducing high-impact foods and food waste can make a meaningful difference.

🛍️

Shopping

Buying fewer new items, using products longer and choosing second-hand goods can reduce consumption-related emissions.

🧮

How EcoTrack works

You enter your own habits. The Java backend applies category factors, adds the four categories and saves your personal result to your dashboard.
